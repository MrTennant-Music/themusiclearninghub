# Concept Sort: Musical Periods

Cumulative concepts from the selected qualification and earlier levels, as requested.
N3 and N4 have too few characteristic concepts for a useful sort.

| Level | Concept pool | Cards per round |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| N5 | 11 | 11 |
| H | 31 | 18 |
| AH | 57 | 18 |

Every available period is represented in each round. Higher and AH sample fresh concepts on Play Again; shared links retain the exact sampled round. Higher combines Medieval and Renaissance as Pre-Baroque. Mode or modal and Mass are included in every new Higher round. Advanced Higher retains the six separate periods. N5 has four groups: Baroque, Classical, Romantic and 20th century. Recorder is grouped under Baroque at N5; Renaissance remains available at Advanced Higher.

This tests characteristic historical associations, not whether a concept has ever appeared in another century. Boundary associations are accepted. Broad, non-diagnostic concepts such as chords, ascending, dynamics and generic articulation are excluded. The original Dictionary and its period revision groups are the main curriculum references.

Review flags mistakes without revealing the correct group, as requested.

Reference checks:

- [Recorder in the Renaissance and Baroque](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/the-development-of-the-recorder)

- [Late nineteenth-century Debussy and whole-tone harmony](https://www.metmuseum.org/pt/toah/ht/10/euwf)
