# Advanced Higher Q6(b)(ii): conclusion evidence audit

Reviewed 18 November 2026 against all nine locally supplied official marking instructions, including the separate extended-answer guidance pages (2016–2019 and 2022–2023) and the additional-guidance columns (all years). This audit concerns prose marking only; no score transcription was changed.

## Mark allocation and evidence scope

- 2 marks: both excerpt periods correctly identified and each justified.
- 1 mark: both periods correctly identified, OR one period correctly identified and justified.
- 0 marks otherwise. No half marks. A correct period plus an additional incorrect period for the same excerpt invalidates that identification.
- At least one accepted evidence family must support each identified period. A family containing AND requires every group; alternatives within a group are equivalent. These requirements are year-specific.
- Teacher override confirmed 18 November 2026: supporting evidence must be written in Q6(b)(ii), Conclusion, itself. Although the official schemes permit links to earlier answers, the app deliberately does not import justification from Q6(a) or Q6(b)(i). Shared claims within the Conclusion must actually refer to both excerpts. Earlier final answers, rough work, other questions and the other excerpt cannot supply evidence. The official 0/1/2 bands are otherwise retained; period names alone cannot earn 2 marks.
- Explicit denials/contradictions do not supply positive evidence. Legitimate negative characteristics (without vibrato; no clear phrase structure) remain valid only where listed.
- The variants below are the digital interpretation of the source concepts, not a claim that every paraphrase is printed in the scheme. Existing common musical spelling alternatives remain available. Exact word/phrase matching avoids unrelated fuzzy matches. Further valid prose can still require human judgement; a finite vocabulary is not unrestricted semantic marking.
