Template sources

Ten editable eight-bar lead sheets for Composition Studio. Melody and chord transcriptions were converted into local MIDI and Studio data. Each selection loads the tune’s key and tempo, eight bars of chords and melody, and a newly randomised drum pattern. The drum and bass accompaniments are generated by the Hub.

Pop transcription data: CoCoPops, Claire Arthur and Nathaniel Condit-Schultz (2023), with the McGill Billboard corpus contributors. Data licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0. This data licence does not grant rights to the underlying songs. No lyrics or original recordings are included.

Traditional and classical entries use credited public-domain melodies. Source chord symbols are retained where practical; any new classroom harmonisation, transposition or rhythm adaptation is identified above.
