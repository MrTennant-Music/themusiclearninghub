# Shared music game engine

Crosswords, Word Search, Connections and Concept Sort are the public apps. Their routes are defined in `shared/music-games-apps.js` and generated by `scripts/build-music-games-pages.cjs`.

Run `node scripts/build-music-games-runtime.cjs` for runtime changes, or `node scripts/build-music-games.js` to rebuild the catalogue and pages.

The internal `music-games/` engine, catalogue, media and storage identities remain in use by these apps, Music Dictionary label activities and Millionaire's media picker. Keep their storage keys and compressed QR hashes stable so existing saved collections and current shared links continue to work.

The retired app pages, full activity authoring editor, synthetic homepage navigation and classroom service have been removed. Collection creation and editing remain in the standalone apps.

Current files live under `music-games/`: the shared player and renderers, `activities/` for collection data, `runtime/` for generated browser bundles, `assets/` for game media, `audio/` for feedback clips and `output/` for retained exports. Build and audit scripts use the `music-games` name.

Concept Sort’s original 77 built-in collections and automatic All generator were retired on 15 November 2026. Their IDs are listed in `scripts/concept-sort-retired-collections.json` so old content generators cannot republish them. The saved collection builder remains available under My Collections. The initial screen now generates a fresh board for the chosen level, using Musical Elements (Type 1) only. Boards use the five musical element groups and at most 40 concept-word cards.

`scripts/build-concept-sort-data.cjs` reuses all current Dictionary concepts, Concept Quiz facts and existing Hub literacy terms. `scripts/concept-sort-literacy.cjs` references unchanged literacy symbol assets. `shared/music-games-sort.js` generates and validates source-backed boards; compact QR recipes restore the exact cards, groups and settings. Type 1 accepts all documented element memberships. Types 2 and 3 are disabled.
