# Instrument Families photographs

23 transparent PNG cut-outs for the National 5 Instrument Families collection.
The Dictionary Orchestra inventory supplies the instrument names and four families.
All 23 instruments are included, including instruments introduced before National 5.

Each image is 240 × 200 pixels, palette-compressed to at most 256 colours.
The PNGs are compressed for use in the game; exact sizes are recorded in the manifest.
Transparent padding has alpha zero. No backdrop or people are included.

`manifest.json` records the source, author, licence and adaptation for every image.
Most originals are the existing licensed Orchestra Sort photographs; saxophone
and tambourine sources are linked individually in the manifest. Preserve their
individual licences and attribution when reusing the derivatives.

Background isolation used the built-in image generation tool on a reference
contact sheet. This removed surroundings, people and duplicate side views;
occluded instrument details may have been reconstructed. These are adapted
photographic cut-outs rather than untouched source photographs. Sharp cropped,
resized and compressed the isolated instruments and preserved transparent edges.

The generation prompt is retained in `generation-prompt.txt`.

The follow-up cleanup removed floating fragments around the instruments using
the built-in image tool, then tightly cropped each photo before compression.
Its prompt is retained in `cleanup-prompt.txt`.
