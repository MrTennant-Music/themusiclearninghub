    The Music Learning Hub · Version 2.0 · 2026

    About

    The Music Learning Hub brings together interactive learning resources and classroom tools to help pupils understand music. It develops music literacy alongside a broader understanding of musical concepts through exploration, practice and play.

    Organised by level and topic from National 3 to Advanced Higher, the Hub supports classroom teaching, revision and independent study around Qualifications Scotland music concepts.

    Built by a music teacher, combining classroom experience with AI-assisted development.

    Updates

    Version 2.0 • November 2026

    • Updated the name to The Music Learning Hub, bringing music literacy, listening, games, performing, composing and teaching tools together.
    • Added Escape Room: explore six rooms, solve musical knowledge, literacy and listening clues, and use the answers to unlock each door. Includes object sound effects, hints and medal rewards.
    • Added Music Dictionary from National 3 to Advanced Higher, with definitions, listening guidance, audio and video examples, related concepts, revision groups and links to practice activities.
    • Added Concept Audio Library, a repository of concept audio examples from National 3 to Advanced Higher.
    • Added Exam Trends to the Dictionary, showing how often concepts appear in reviewed listening papers and linking to relevant questions in the Digital Question Papers.
    • Added Digital Question Papers at Advanced Higher level.
    • Expanded Practice Questions with exam-style literacy tasks, audio, marking feedback, worksheets and a full-screen score view for longer questions.
    • Added Keyboard Note Identification from National 3 to Advanced Higher. Name highlighted keys or match written notes to keys, with level-specific options and printable worksheets.
    • Added Crosswords, Connections, Word Search, Concept Sort and Concept Charades, available from the Games section.
    • Added Composition Studio for creating and playing chord progressions, melodies and drum patterns.
    • Added Digital Performance Assessment for recording performing assessments, feedback and marks.
    • Added Interactive Piano for playing notes and chords with a mouse, touchscreen or computer keyboard.
    • Added Classroom Timer with countdowns, stopwatches and custom labels for classroom activities.
    • Added Stave Paper Generator for creating and downloading blank and piano manuscript paper.
    • Added Chromatic Tuner with live pitch feedback and guided string tuning for guitar, bass guitar, ukulele and violin.
    • Experimental: Host a Game in Keyboard Note Identification. Participants can join by QR code and compete in a Quiz or Race. This feature is still being tested and may change.
    • Improved navigation, activity controls, notation, accessibility and the fitted desktop layout across the Hub.
    • Bug fixes and other improvements across the Hub.

    Version 1.1 • October 2026

    • Added and expanded Ear Training (formerly Aural Recognition) from National 3 to Advanced Higher, with customisable listening exercises, generated musical excerpts and choice or typed answers.
    • Added Family Fortunes from National 3 to Advanced Higher, with two-team play, face-offs, play or pass, steals, scoring and editable rounds.
    • Added Metronome with adjustable tempo, tap tempo, beats per bar, accents and subdivisions.
    • Bug fixes and other improvements across the Hub.

    Version 1.0 • August 2026

    • Initial release of The Music Learning Hub.
