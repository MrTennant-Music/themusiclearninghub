Changes for The Music Learning Hub, 29 October 2026:
Extracted selected MP3 notes from the instrument bundles; converted to mono
32 kHz AAC at 64 kbps; retained every third semitone from MIDI 36 to 96.
The browser transposes nearest samples and crossfades a short sustain loop
for organ, strings, flute, brass, synth and pad.
